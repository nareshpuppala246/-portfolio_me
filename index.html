About

Embedded engineer focused on reliable, shippable firmware.

I’m Naresh Puppala, an Embedded Systems Engineer with 7+ years designing and developing production-grade firmware for industrial, IoT, and smart-device products.

I work across the product lifecycle: firmware architecture, BSP and SDK integration, hardware bring-up, real-time systems, system debugging, and optimization. Day-to-day platforms include Infineon PSoC Edge E84, STM32, ESP32, Cypress PSoC, and ARM Cortex-M.

Recent focus includes edge AI and embedded vision on PSoC Edge E84 (multi-camera, JPEG path, DeepCraft Studio), plus wireless connectivity, low-power design, and battery-managed products with SPI Flash logging and fuel-gauge integration.

Core strengths RTOS firmware, peripheral drivers, wireless stacks, HW/FW debug, and field reliability.
Current focus Infineon PSoC Edge E84, edge AI / vision, GPS/GSM/BLE/LoRa, and power-aware devices.
Based in Hyderabad, India — open to firmware / embedded / IoT engineering roles.
